## 4. Transition Mechanics & Implementation

### Transition 1: Scene 01 -> Scene 02 (The Portal Shift)
* **Style**: Directional Whip-Zoom + Light Sweep
* **Duration**: 8 frames (0.33 sec)
* **Technique**:
  * Set a keyframe on Scene 01 scale from `100%` to `125%` over 8 frames with ease-in curve.
  * Add a horizontal directional blur (`angle: 0°`, `strength: 25`).
  * Scene 02 enters at `115%` scale decelerating to `100%` with ease-out curve.
  * Apply a subtle warm tungsten lens flare overlay set to `Screen` blend mode at 35% opacity.

### Transition 2: Scene 02 -> Scene 03 (Command to Dossier)
* **Style**: Smooth Lateral Push Pan
* **Duration**: 8 frames (0.33 sec)
* **Technique**:
  * Push Scene 02 horizontally to the left (`X: 960 -> -960`) while Scene 03 enters from the right (`X: 2880 -> 960`).
  * Apply motion blur with a `180° shutter angle` for smooth optical physics.

### Transition 3: Scene 03 -> Scene 04 (Dossier to Rehearsal Chamber)
* **Style**: Macro Snap-Zoom (Rack-Focus Match)
* **Duration**: 8 frames (0.33 sec)
* **Technique**:
  * Rapid scale zoom into the speaker avatar on Geoffrey Anyona's card.
  * Apply Gaussian Blur (`radius: 0 to 18`) on Scene 03 tail.
  * Scene 04 emerges from blur (`radius: 18 to 0`) centered squarely on the 145 WPM cadence metric.

### Transition 4: Scene 04 -> Outro (The Sovereign Mark)
* **Style**: Hard Cut on Impact
* **Duration**: Instantaneous (0 frames)
* **Technique**:
  * Cut abruptly to solid obsidian black (`#0B0F17`) at frame 480 (`00:20:00`).
  * Exactly on frame 480, trigger the debate gavel strike audio waveform peak.

## 6. Color Grading & Finishing Node Tree

To ensure visual unity across all 4 Veo renders, apply this universal color grade in DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Lumetri Color:

1. **Node 1 (Exposure & Contrast)**:
   * Lift: `+0.015` (prevents crushed blacks, preserves deep obsidian tones)
   * Gamma: `0.000`
   * Gain: `0.985` (protects clean ivory whites from digital clipping)
2. **Node 2 (Editorial Temperature)**:
   * White Balance: `5600K` neutral daylight base
   * Tint: `+2.0` (eliminates sickly digital green tint)
   * Warm Highlight Push: Subtle amber-gold lift in highlights (`Hue: 42°`, `Sat: 6%`)
3. **Node 3 (Sharpening & Texture)**:
   * Radius: `0.48`
   * Softness: `0.52`
   * Organic 35mm film grain at `12% opacity` (fine grain size) to eliminate digital compression banding.
